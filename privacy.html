Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy covers the website leviathandecoded.github.io and leviathan-studio, a private publishing tool the Leviathan Decoded channel uses to manage its own social media accounts.

What leviathan-studio accesses

  • YouTube (Google APIs): only the Leviathan Decoded channel's own account, to upload its own videos, captions and thumbnails, manage its playlists and read its own channel analytics.
  • Instagram and Facebook (Meta APIs): only the Leviathan Decoded Instagram account and Facebook Page, to publish its own posts and read their comments and statistics.

The tool is used only by the channel owner. It does not access, collect or store data about any other user, and it is not offered to the public.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored only in the owner's local device keychain. They are never shared, sold or sent to any third party, and the tool does not use them for advertising. Analytics of the channel's own content are kept locally to plan future videos.

Google API Services

leviathan-studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploads use the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions or Meta's Business integrations settings.

This website

This site sets no cookies and has no analytics or trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (see GitHub's privacy statement).
