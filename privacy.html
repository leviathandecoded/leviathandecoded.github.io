Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy covers the website leviathandecoded.github.io and leviathan-studio, the private publishing tool the Leviathan Decoded channel uses to manage its own YouTube channel, Instagram account and Facebook Page. leviathan-studio has a single user: the channel owner. It is not offered to the public.

1. Data leviathan-studio accesses, and why

Google / YouTube (only the Leviathan Decoded channel, after the owner signs in and consents):

  • youtube.upload: upload the channel's own videos and Shorts.
  • youtube: set titles, descriptions, thumbnails, publish times and playlists of the channel's own videos.
  • youtube.force-ssl: upload captions for the channel's own videos, and read and reply to comments on them.
  • yt-analytics.readonly: read the channel's own analytics (views, watch time, subscribers gained) to plan future videos.

Meta (only the Leviathan Decoded Instagram account and Facebook Page): publish the account's own Reels and Stories, read comments on them, and read their statistics.

The data involved is the channel's own content and metadata, comments that viewers post publicly on the channel's videos (commenter display name and text), and aggregate analytics for the channel. leviathan-studio does not access any other account, private messages, contacts, or personal data of viewers beyond those public comments.

2. How the data is used

Only to publish and manage the channel's own content, reply to comments, and decide what to make next. It is never used for advertising, never sold, never used to build profiles of viewers, never used to train AI models, and never used to generate images of people.

3. Storage, retention and deletion

  • Google access tokens are stored only in the owner's local device keychain.
  • The Meta Page token is stored in the owner's keychain and, for scheduled Instagram posting, as an encrypted GitHub Actions secret in the owner's private repository.
  • Analytics summaries of the channel's own content are kept in the owner's private repository for up to 24 months, then deleted.
  • Comments are read through the API when replying and are not stored.

Tokens are deleted when access is revoked or the tool is retired.

4. Sharing

No data obtained through Google or Meta APIs is shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party.

5. Google API Services

leviathan-studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; by using them you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies.

6. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions or Meta's Business integrations settings. Revoking stops all access immediately.

7. This website

This site needs no login, sets no cookies and has no analytics or trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (see GitHub's privacy statement).

8. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: leviathandecoded@gmail.com
