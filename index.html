About leviathan-studio (our Google API app)

leviathan-studio is the private publishing tool behind this channel. It signs in only to the Leviathan Decoded YouTube channel, with the channel owner's consent, and does four things:

  • Uploads the channel's own videos and Shorts, with their captions and thumbnails, and schedules when they go public.
  • Organizes those videos into the channel's playlists.
  • Replies to comments on the channel's own videos.
  • Reads the channel's own YouTube Analytics (views, watch time) to plan future videos.

It never accesses other people's accounts or data, it is not offered to the public, and it is not used to generate images of people. This page needs no login. Details on what data the app uses are in the privacy policy.

Ground rules

Labs, fictional apps and publicly disclosed, patched vulnerabilities only. Every attack is shown together with its fix. Only test systems you own or are authorized to test.
